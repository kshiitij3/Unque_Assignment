Privacy Policy

LeadFlow Leads PoC · Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how LeadFlow Leads PoC handles information submitted through Meta lead forms for demonstration and testing of a real-time lead delivery prototype.

Information we collect

When a person submits a lead form, the prototype may receive the information entered in that form, such as name, email address, and phone number. It may also process technical lead metadata supplied by Meta, such as a lead identifier, form identifier, Page identifier, or submission timestamp.

How we use information

Information is used only to demonstrate, test, troubleshoot, and operate the LeadFlow Leads PoC: receiving Meta webhook notifications, retrieving the associated lead details, storing the record in the project's database, and displaying it in the connected test application. Do not submit sensitive personal information through this demonstration form.

Storage and sharing

Lead records are processed by the project's backend and stored in its configured database. The prototype does not sell lead information. Data may pass through Meta's systems and the hosting or tunnelling services used to operate the demonstration. Access should be limited to people administering the prototype.

Retention and deletion

Because this is a proof of concept, lead information should be retained only for as long as needed for testing and demonstration. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information submitted to this prototype, contact the project administrator using the email below. The administrator will review the request and remove the corresponding test record where applicable.

Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect the prototype, including keeping access tokens and application secrets out of the mobile app and source repository. This is a demonstration project and should not be used to collect sensitive or high-risk information.

Children

This demonstration is not intended for children, and it should not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the prototype changes. The date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.
